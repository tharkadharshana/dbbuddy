Two-brand embed test

One app, one database, two partner keys. Simulates each partner's backoffice building its own iframe URL.
Each brand needs an app_access_token from its own backoffice — Salesplay's from predev2, Sellmo's from predev1. They are not interchangeable: a predev1 token against Salesplay's key resolves to a different provider instance and will fail. Read it from DevTools → Application → Cookies → app_access_token on that backoffice.
